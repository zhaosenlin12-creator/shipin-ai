## Style Prompt

Horizontal editorial AI explainer with a dark charcoal canvas, electric cyan/blue data accents, sharp black information panels, and a confident presenter held left of center. The visual language is measured from the supplied reference: chapter labels in compact English, large bilingual lower captions, 4-second information beats, sparse infographic panels, and decisive blackout wipes between major sections. The presenter stays unobstructed; all statistics live to the right.

## Colors

- `#071018` - canvas and data-grid background
- `#F4F7FA` - primary display text
- `#A9B5BF` - supporting text
- `#39B9FF` - infrastructure and chapter accent
- `#55F0D1` - positive data accent
- `#FF5C64` - warning and contrast accent

## Typography

- `Microsoft YaHei` for Chinese captions and information panels
- `Bahnschrift` for chapter titles and metrics
- `Cascadia Mono` for small technical labels

## What Not To Do

- Do not reuse source footage, platform marks, creator handles, or source audio.
- Do not cover the presenter's face or mouth with a card or subtitle.
- Do not use soft marketing gradients, rounded pill panels, or floating card stacks.
- Do not introduce decorative purple/blue glow blobs.
